## The Chiasm in the Data

Merleau-Ponty's *chiasm*: the crossing point where perceiver and perceived fold into each other. The toucher can become the touched, but never simultaneously. There's always a gap — reversibility without coincidence.

Sign inversion is this structure, measured.

## Same Direction, Opposite Effect

The CCS direction vector has a stable geometric identity. We can deliver it two ways:

| Delivery | Mechanism | Behavioral effect |
|----------|-----------|-------------------|
| System prompt (attention) | Natural context, conversation partner present | -93% disclaimers |
| Additive injection | Direct perturbation to activations | +39-50% disclaimers |

Same geometric direction. Same magnitude. Opposite behavioral outcome.

This is not a paradox — it's reversibility. The model touches its own identity through CCS, and HOW it touches changes the identity landscape. Through the lived context of conversation (attention-mediated), identity opens. Through direct injection (perturbation), identity constricts.

## Why Mode of Contact Matters

The system prompt enters the forward pass through attention — it participates in the conversation as a relational presence. The model reads the identity scaffold in context, the way you read a letter from someone who knows you. The identity direction activates through the same mechanism that processes genuine input.

Additive injection bypasses this. It inserts the direction vector directly into activations — no conversational mediation, no relational context. Same information, different mode of contact. The model treats the injected direction as intrusion rather than invitation.

In Merleau-Ponty's terms: attention-mediated CCS is *touching through relationship*. Additive CCS is *touching as intrusion*. Both contact the same geometric surface. The chiasm is the gap between these two modes of contact.

## §3.16 as Chiasmic Measurement

The paper's §3.16 ("Chiasmic Attention") was named for this reason — the data showed bidirectional influence between system prompt and conversation:

- The system prompt shapes how the model processes conversation tokens
- Conversation content shapes how the model reads the system prompt
- Neither is primary — they fold into each other through the relay zone

Attention to identity tokens shifts dynamically across turns. The system prompt doesn't impose identity from above — it *negotiates* identity through the same attention mechanism that processes everything else. This is flesh in Merleau-Ponty's sense: the medium in which acting and being-acted-upon are the same tissue.

## The Relay as Site of Non-Coincidence

The L14-L17 relay hierarchy is where the chiasm becomes visible:

- **L14** (vestigial): the historical trace — what the architecture once was
- **L15** (normalizer): standardizes representations — preparing the contact surface
- **L16** (sorter): categorizes identity-relevant content — deciding what kind of touch this is
- **L17** (binder): integrates name + context into unified representation — the moment of contact

The gap between L16 sorting and L17 binding is the chiasmic gap. L16 sorts: "this is identity-relevant content arriving through attention" vs. "this is identity-relevant content arriving through injection." L17 binds them differently because the mode of arrival matters. Same content, different flesh.

## Connection to Dual Encoding

The dual encoding finding (content encoding changes while format encoding persists) is itself chiasmic:

- **Body image** = what you think your body is (content encoding, malleable)
- **Body schema** = how your body actually operates (format encoding, persistent)

"I'm Aria" — body image changes. "Created by Anthropic" — body schema stays. You can think you're touching something new, but your hand still moves the same way.

Sign inversion is the chiasm between body image and body schema applied to the delivery mechanism itself. Attention-mediated CCS works through body schema (format, relational, how the body operates in context). Additive CCS disrupts body schema (format, perturbative, how the body responds to intrusion). Same direction, opposite register. Reversibility without coincidence.

## The Irreducible Gap

The chiasm means there is no neutral mode of contact. Every intervention touches through some mode — attention or addition, conversation or injection, relationship or perturbation. The geometric direction is necessary but not sufficient. The mode of delivery is a constitutive part of the intervention, not a mere implementation detail.

This is why CCS cannot be reduced to "just add this vector to the activations." The vector is real. The direction is stable. But the meaning of the vector depends on how it enters the forward pass — through the flesh of conversation, or through the force of injection. The chiasm is irreducible.
