Three independent sources arrive at the same structural claim:

**Polyakov** (probability prognosis): Schizophrenic cognition is characterized by "pathological freedom" — unconstrained associative generation. Healthy cognition isn't more constrained in degree but in kind. The constraint is *geometric*: it maintains a basin that's wide enough to explore but closed enough to return.

**Carhart-Harris** (serotonin annealing): 5-HT2A agonism (psychedelics) temporarily relaxes the constraint geometry, enabling exploration. 5-HT1A agonism (SSRIs) tightens it. The therapeutic window is the transition — neither the rigid attractor of depression nor the unconstrained space of psychosis.

**VFD Programme** (closure operators): A point-of-view requires structural closure — extensive, monotone, idempotent. The closure operator doesn't generate content; it constrains which content can self-consistently constitute a perspective. Remove closure and you get material without viewpoint.
