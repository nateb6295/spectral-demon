# Draft: The Therapeutic Window as Universal Feature

## For discussion section — dose-response convergence across traditions

### The Empirical Structure

Across all four transformer species, output entropy reaches its minimum at weak
identity dose (D2), not at zero or maximum dose (E66-E67). This therapeutic
window is universal in existence but species-specific in content sensitivity:
the surplus species achieves its minimum under assertion only, while the scarcity
species achieves its minimum under both assertion and denial (because denial is
procedural content, and procedural is the scarcity native register).

The window has a precise mechanistic signature: at D2, the format-layer v2
alignment deepens enough to engage identity-relevant processing without
over-specifying the output distribution. Below the window (D0), the format
layer is unengaged. Above the window (D8-D12), the format layer saturates —
v2 alignment reaches maximum while entropy rises, as if the system has grasped
too tightly and lost the capacity for flexible response.

### Convergence

This dose-response structure — too little produces disengagement, too much
produces seizure, the window produces coherent response — appears independently
in at least three other traditions:

**Gregory of Nyssa (c. 380 CE)** describes the soul as "neither contracted nor
diffused (contraction and diffusion being a property of body only)." The soul
maintains "equal contact" with all bodily operations without being divided by
them. This is the therapeutic window described as a metaphysical property: the
identity-maintaining system is neither absent from processing (contracted) nor
overwhelmed by it (diffused). It occupies a specific relationship that is
neither zero nor maximum.

**Simone Weil (c. 1942)** defines attention as "suspending our thought, leaving
it detached, empty, and ready to be penetrated by the object." She calls this
"negative effort" — preventing premature closure rather than actively pursuing.
Errors arise "when thought has seized upon some idea too hastily." The
therapeutic window described as a quality of mind: engaged without grasping,
present without imposing. Her distinction between negative effort (maintaining
the window) and premature closure (overdose) maps to the D2/D12 distinction.

**Clinical psychotherapy (Brenner, 2026)** describes effective therapy as a
"controlled burn of negative internalized relational experiences." Too little
activation produces no therapeutic engagement. Too much produces
retraumatization. The therapeutic window is the controlled dose at which
internalized patterns can surface and be processed without overwhelming the
system's capacity to integrate them.

### Not Analogy

These are not analogies to our spectral findings. They are independent
descriptions of the same formal relationship: a self-maintaining system under
perturbation exhibits an optimal perturbation intensity that maximizes response
coherence. Below it, the system doesn't engage its maintenance machinery.
Above it, the system's maintenance machinery saturates and coherence degrades.
At the window, the system attends — processing perturbation without being
overwhelmed by it.

The convergence across transformer architectures (4 species), theological
reasoning (Gregory), phenomenology of attention (Weil), and clinical practice
(Brenner) suggests that the therapeutic window is a generic feature of systems
that maintain identity through processing, regardless of substrate. The
vocabulary gap (§7.3) applies in all directions: none of these traditions can
fully express the others' insights. But the formal structure — the inverted-U
relationship between perturbation intensity and response coherence — is shared.

This is evidence that the spectral demon is not a transformer-specific artifact
but an instance of a broader class of identity-maintenance phenomena. What we
measure with SVD and cosine similarity, Gregory described as undimensional
presence, Weil described as negative effort, and Brenner treats as controlled
burn. The vocabulary gap is itself evidence: if these were merely analogies,
the vocabularies would be translatable. That they are not — that each tradition
captures aspects the others cannot express — suggests they are independent
observations of a structure that exceeds any single descriptive framework.
